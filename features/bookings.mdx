## Overview

Bookings allow players to reserve court time at venues. Hub supports two types of bookings:

1. **Direct Bookings**: Individual players booking courts for personal use
2. **Match Bookings**: Court reservations created through the match system with split payments

## Booking Status

Understand what each booking status means:

### Active Statuses

<Accordion title="PENDING_PAYMENT">
  **Description**: Booking created but payment not yet completed
  
  **What it means**:
  - The time slot is temporarily held for you
  - You have 30 minutes to complete payment
  - The booking will automatically cancel if not paid
  
  **What you can do**:
  - Complete payment to confirm
  - Let it expire to release the slot
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="CONFIRMED">
  **Description**: Payment completed and booking active
  
  **What it means**:
  - Your court is reserved
  - You can access the venue at the scheduled time
  - The booking appears in your upcoming reservations
  
  **What you can do**:
  - Cancel up to 24 hours before (get refund)
  - View booking details
  - Check in at the venue
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="PENDING_MATCH">
  **Description**: Booking created for a match, waiting for all players to join
  
  **What it means**:
  - Part of a match request system
  - The organizer has paid their portion
  - Waiting for other players to confirm
  
  **What you can do**:
  - View match details
  - Share invitation link
  - Wait for match to fill
</Accordion>

### Final Statuses

<Accordion title="CANCELLED">
  **Description**: Booking was cancelled by player or admin
  
  **What it means**:
  - The time slot is released
  - Refund initiated if payment was made
  - Appears in booking history
  
  **Why it happens**:
  - Player cancelled (more than 24h before)
  - Admin cancelled
  - Payment timeout
  - Match cancelled or expired
</Accordion>

## Cancellation Policy

### Player-Initiated Cancellation

<Warning>
  Bookings can only be cancelled **more than 24 hours** before the scheduled start time.
</Warning>

#### How to Cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Bookings">
    Go to "Mis Reservas" from your dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Your Booking">
    Locate the booking you want to cancel in the "Upcoming" tab
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel">
    Click the "Cancelar" button on the booking card
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Cancellation">
    Review the cancellation notice and optionally provide a reason
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Request">
    Confirm to process the cancellation and refund
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Cancellation Restrictions

<Note>
  **24-Hour Policy**: Cancellations within 24 hours of the scheduled start time are not allowed to protect venue revenue and planning.
</Note>

If you try to cancel within 24 hours, you'll see:

> "Bookings cannot be cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled start time"

### Refund Processing

When you successfully cancel:

- **Status** changes to `CANCELLED`
- **Payment Status** updates to `REFUNDED`
- **Refund** is initiated automatically
- **Time Slot** is released for other players
- **Email Notification** confirms the cancellation

### Admin Cancellation

Venue owners or administrators can cancel bookings at any time with a reason. Admin cancellations:

- Issue automatic refunds for paid bookings
- Include an explanation in the cancellation reason
- Cannot be reversed

### Booking Hold System

When you initiate a booking:

1. **Slot Reserved**: Time slot is temporarily held
2. **30-Minute Timer**: You have 30 minutes to complete payment
3. **Payment Page**: Redirected to secure payment interface
4. **Auto-Expiry**: Unpaid bookings automatically cancel after 30 minutes
5. **Slot Release**: Failed/expired bookings free the time slot immediately

<Tip>
  Complete payment promptly to secure your booking. High-demand slots may be taken by others if your payment expires.
</Tip>

## Match Bookings

Match bookings work differently from direct bookings:

### Split Payment System

When creating a match:

1. **Organizer Books**: Court is reserved with a match request
2. **Organizer Pays First**: Pays their portion (e.g., 1/4 for 2v2)
3. **Status**: Booking set to `PENDING_MATCH`
4. **Players Join**: Other players join and pay their share
5. **Match Confirms**: When full, booking moves to `CONFIRMED`
6. **Payment Records**: Separate payment record for each player

### Match Booking Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Match Created">
    Organizer creates match and booking enters PENDING_MATCH status
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organizer Pays">
    Organizer completes their portion of the payment
  </Step>
  <Step title="Players Join">
    Other players join the match and pay their shares
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match Full">
    When all spots are filled, booking confirms automatically
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match Time">
    Players show up and play at the scheduled time
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If a match doesn't fill or is cancelled, the booking is automatically cancelled and all payments are refunded.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

<Accordion title="My booking expired before I could pay">
  **Solution**: The 30-minute payment window ensures fair access to courts. Try booking again and complete payment immediately. Consider saving your payment method for faster checkout.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't cancel my booking">
  **Check**: Are you within 24 hours of the start time? If yes, cancellation is not allowed per platform policy. Contact the venue directly for special circumstances.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Payment succeeded but booking shows pending">
  **Solution**: Wait a few seconds and refresh the page. If the issue persists, check your email for confirmation. Payment webhooks may have a slight delay.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The slot shows available but booking fails">
  **Reason**: Another player may have booked it just before you. Availability is checked in real-time at the moment of booking. Try selecting a different time slot.
</Accordion>
