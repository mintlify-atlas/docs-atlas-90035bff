## Welcome to Hub

As a player on Hub, you can discover venues, book padel courts, find match partners, and manage your playing schedule. This guide covers everything you need to know.

---

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    Navigate to **Settings** from your dashboard to update your profile information:
    
    - Add your skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional)
    - Upload a profile photo
    - Set your preferred playing location
    - Configure notification preferences
    
    <Tip>A complete profile helps other players find you for matches and builds trust in the community.</Tip>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Explore Venues">
    Browse available venues in your area from the dashboard. You can:
    
    - View venues on a map
    - Search by location and radius
    - See venue details, amenities, and court availability
    - View photos and reviews
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Make Your First Booking">
    Select a venue, choose an available time slot, and complete your booking with secure payment processing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Booking Courts

### Finding Available Slots

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Browse Venues" icon="location-dot">
    Use the venue search to find courts near you. Filter by distance, price range, and amenities.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Check Availability" icon="calendar">
    View real-time slot availability for any date. Available slots are shown in green.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Creating a Booking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Venue">
    Click on any venue to view its resources (courts) and detailed information.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Date and Time">
    Select your preferred date and click on an available time slot. Each slot shows:
    
    - Start and end time
    - Duration (typically 60 or 90 minutes)
    - Price for that time slot
    - Court type and features
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm and Pay">
    Review your booking details and complete payment securely. You'll receive:
    
    - Instant booking confirmation
    - Email receipt
    - Calendar invitation (if enabled)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Payment is processed immediately. Cancellation policies vary by venue - check the venue's terms before booking.
</Note>

### Managing Your Bookings

Access all your bookings from **Dashboard > Bookings**:

- **Upcoming**: View all confirmed future bookings
- **Past**: Review booking history
- **Cancel**: Cancel bookings according to the venue's policy

<Tip>Add bookings to your calendar by clicking the "Add to Calendar" button in your booking details.</Tip>

## Match Making

Hub's intelligent match-making system helps you find players at your level for competitive games.

### How Match Making Works

<Accordion title="The Match-Making Flow">
  1. **Search for Slots**: Find available courts that fit your schedule and location
  2. **Create Match Request**: Book a slot and specify match format (2v2, 1v1) and skill level
  3. **Payment Hold**: Your payment is held securely until the match is confirmed
  4. **Find Players**: The system sends invitations to players matching your criteria
  5. **Match Confirmed**: Once all spots are filled, your match is confirmed
  6. **Play & Check-in**: Show up and check in using your phone's GPS
</Accordion>

### Creating a Match Request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search Available Match Slots">
    From the dashboard, click **Find a Match**. Set your search criteria:
    
    - **Location**: Enter your location or use GPS
    - **Date & Time**: When you want to play
    - **Duration**: Typical match length (60-90 minutes)
    - **Format**: 2v2 (doubles) or 1v1 (singles)
    - **Skill Level**: Your playing level
    
    The system will show all available slots matching your criteria.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select a Slot and Create Request">
    Choose your preferred slot and click **Create Match Request**. You'll need to:
    
    - Confirm the booking details
    - Add an optional message to potential players
    - Complete payment (held until match is confirmed)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Wait for Players">
    The system automatically sends invitations to players in your area who match your skill level. You'll receive notifications as players accept.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Match Confirmed">
    Once all spots are filled, your match is confirmed and all players receive:
    
    - Match details and court location
    - Opponent profiles
    - Check-in instructions
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If the match isn't filled within 24 hours of the booking time, your payment will be automatically refunded.
</Note>

### Responding to Match Invitations

Receive invitations from other players organizing matches:

1. **View Invitation**: Check match details, location, time, and organizer profile
2. **Accept or Decline**: Respond to the invitation
3. **Join the Match**: If accepted, complete payment and you're in

<Tip>Respond to invitations quickly - spots fill fast for popular time slots!</Tip>

### Managing Your Matches

From **Dashboard > Matches**, you can:

- View all upcoming matches
- See participant profiles
- Check match location and time
- Cancel (with restrictions) or report absence
- View match history

### Match Policies

<Accordion title="Cancellation Policy">
  - **48+ hours before**: Full refund, leave match freely
  - **24-48 hours before**: May incur cancellation fee
  - **Under 24 hours**: Report absence to find substitute, fees apply
  - **No-show**: Full charge + potential account restriction
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Skill Level Guidelines">
  - **Beginner**: New to padel, learning basics
  - **Intermediate**: Comfortable with rules, developing strategy
  - **Advanced**: Competitive player, strong technique
  - **Professional**: Tournament-level play
  
  <Tip>Be honest about your skill level - it ensures better matches for everyone!</Tip>
</Accordion>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="I can't see any available slots">
  **Possible solutions:**
  
  - Expand your search radius
  - Try different dates or times
  - Check if you've filtered by price or specific venue
  - Verify the venue is active (some venues have seasonal hours)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My booking payment failed">
  **Check the following:**
  
  - Card has sufficient funds
  - Card details are correct
  - Card is enabled for online transactions
  - Try a different payment method
  - Contact your bank if the issue persists
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I haven't received any match invitations">
  **Try these steps:**
  
  - Verify your skill level is set correctly
  - Expand your search radius in preferences
  - Ensure notifications are enabled
  - Check your spam folder for invitation emails
  - Try creating your own match request
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I need to cancel a confirmed match">
  - Navigate to **Dashboard > Matches**
  - Click on the match to view details
  - Click **Report Absence** to notify other players
  - The system will attempt to find a substitute
  - Cancellation fees may apply based on timing
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Check-in isn't working">
  **Verify:**
  
  - Location services are enabled on your device
  - You're within 100 meters of the venue
  - GPS signal is strong (try moving outside if indoors)
  - You're checking in within the allowed time window
</Accordion>
